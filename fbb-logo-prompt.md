Design a modern, minimalist lettermark logo using the monogram "FBB" for "Friends Behind Bars" — a company that helps families send approved, uplifting photos to incarcerated loved ones. The logo should feel warm, hopeful, and trustworthy — about connection and staying in touch, not punishment or incarceration imagery. Use an American flag color palette: white as the primary background, deep navy blue (#0A3161) as the main letterform/structure color, and crimson red (#B31942) as a small accent (underline, dot, or highlight on one letter). Clean, bold, geometric sans-serif letterforms, flat vector style, no gradients or photorealism. The mark should read clearly at small sizes (favicon/app icon scale) and work equally well as a standalone icon or paired with a wordmark. Contained in a simple circular or rounded-square badge. Transparent background. Avoid literal jail bars, cages, barbed wire, or handcuffs.
